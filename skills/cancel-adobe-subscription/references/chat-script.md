# Adobe support chat — the exact messages

Send in this order; wait for the rep between steps. **Agents: fill brackets only with what the account page shows or what the user told you in this conversation. Do not invent a reason. Show the user any message not on this page before sending it.**

## 1. Case message (post while queued — asks, doesn't instruct)

> Hi. On account **[e-mail shown on the plans page]**: I'm looking at cancelling my **[Plan name]** (annual, paid monthly) and would like to know my options and whether the early-termination fee can be waived. **[Only if true and taken from the page: The plan has been discontinued / The price rose from X to Y at renewal.] [Only if the user gave one, in their words: their reason.]** Please don't process anything yet — I'll confirm before you do. Thank you.

If a fee was already charged via the website today, append:

> I was charged an early-termination fee of **[amount]** on **[date]**; could that charge be cancelled or refunded?

If the user gave no reason, leave that clause out — the request works without it.

## 2. Retention offer → decline

> Thank you, but no — I want to stop the plan, not extend it. Could the fee be waived on the cancellation?

## 3. Fee stated without a waiver → ask once

> Could you waive the early-termination fee as a one-time exception? I'd rather not leave on a bad note.

## 4. Passcode

Rep: "a one-time passcode has been sent to your email" (subject: *One Time Passcode for Account Verification*, usually within a minute). **The user types it into the chat.** While waiting:

> One moment please.

## 5. Lock-in — only after the user has said "go ahead" (hard rule 5)

> Just to confirm before you finalize: the cancellation is with the early termination fee waived — nothing charged, no fee in any amount — correct? Please don't process it if any fee would apply.

Wait for **"That is correct."** (or equivalent).

## 6. Close out

> Could you give me the case number for this chat, and confirm no fee will be charged to my card? Thank you for your help today.

Case numbers look like `ADB-XXXXXXXX-XXXX`. **Don't skip this.** The chat transcript disappears when the window closes; the case number is Adobe's official reference for what was agreed — your proof, in their system. Note it with the date and the rep's first name, for your own records; keep the transcript to yourself — it names an identifiable person.

If a fee had been raised earlier, the rep may say it "was not charged" — that means it was cancelled before settlement, the best outcome.

## If the waiver is refused twice — only after the user has said "go ahead" to end-of-term

> Understood. Then please set the plan to cancel at the end of the current term with no further charges, and confirm that in writing.
